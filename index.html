The sentence

For the most fragile places

  • Limit the number of visitors
  • Stop geotagging fragile sites
  • Control permits for the highest mountains more strictly

For each of us

  • Stay on the trails
  • Take your rubbish home
  • Never light a fire in the wild
  • Sometimes choose a less famous place

Social media is not the only guilty party, but it is a powerful accelerator. In just a few days, it can turn a fragile place into a viral spot.
